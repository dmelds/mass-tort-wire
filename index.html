NEWS INTEL AI
TRACKED TOPICS

Each topic runs as a Google News search, up to 20. AI topics search for the AI Wire, Intelligencer topics for the INTELLIGENCER tab, and the rest for the News Wire. A story that covers an AI or Intelligencer topic shows in that tab. ALL shows every tab together.

OUTLETS SEARCHED FOR YOUR TOPICS (ONE DOMAIN PER LINE)

Each outlet runs one Google News search limited to that site, and requires a topic term in the headline. Leave blank to turn this off.

TRUSTED SOURCES (ONE PER LINE)

A topic search keeps an item when its headline or summary carries the topic. These publishers are exempt, so a Reuters story headlined "forever chemicals" still counts as PFAS. A publisher name as shown on items, or a domain.

BLOCKED SOURCES (ONE PER LINE)

A publisher name as shown on items, or a domain. Applies to both channels.

BLOCKED WORDS (ONE PER LINE)

A story whose headline or summary uses one of these words or phrases is hidden from every channel. Plurals and -ed/-ing forms count too, so "copyright" also hides "copyrighted". Join words with + to hide a story only when it uses all of them: "police + AI" hides police AI stories and leaves other police news.

NEWS SITE FEEDS (ONE PER LINE)
FIRMS TO WATCH (ONE PER LINE)

Each firm runs a Google News search for its exact name on the News Wire: verdicts, filings, appointments, press.

FIRM FEEDS (ONE PER LINE)

A blog page URL works if the page lists its feed. Settings save to the site, so every window and device shares them.

AI SEARCHES (ONE PER LINE)

Each line runs as its own Google News search for the AI Wire and tags whatever it returns. Quotes force a word to appear, and intitle: forces it into the headline. Google News ignores allintitle:.

AI FEEDS (ONE PER LINE)

Publications that cover AI in law practice. These run unfiltered, so everything they publish in the window arrives.
