#   topic | page page page ...

# The topic is the name as it appears in the wire's TRACKED TOPICS, without any
# press aliases after "=". Matching ignores case. Pages are full URLs, separated
# by spaces; the first page listed is the one the report leads with, so put the
# updates page first and the evergreen pages after it. A topic on the wire with
# no line here is listed at the foot of the report under "topics with no page",
# so adding a topic to the wire shows up here as a reminder.
#
# A line starting with "-" names a topic the wire tracks that feeds no page by
# design (an Intelligencer beat, say), so the report stops listing it as unmapped.
#
# Docket entries carry their own pages from docket/cases.txt and need no line.
# A page cases.txt names that no topic line carries gets a section of its own in
# the report, one per page, so a case with two pages reports twice; list such
# pages on the topic they belong to and the entries fold into that section.
#
